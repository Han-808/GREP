THE IDEA

Beyond a plausible first impression.

Generated rooms can appear convincing while containing collisions, inconsistent object scales, or furniture that cannot be used as intended. GREP evaluates room layouts through physical validity, visual coherence, and human-use logic, connecting findings to the objects, relationships, and visual evidence behind them.

The goal is an interpretable evaluation: what went wrong, where it happened, and why it matters.

THREE COMPLEMENTARY VIEWS

Grounded in principles.

Physical validity

Collision · Support · Bounds

Can the objects occupy their positions and remain physically supported?

Visual coherence

Scale · Style · Pairing

Do the selected objects form a visually coherent room?

Human-use logic

Function · Placement

Can people access and use the objects in the intended setting?
