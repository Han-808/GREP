Principles guide the questions

GREP inspects objects and relationships in the generated room, including those absent from the input description. p. 2 ↗

The principles, metrics, and check types stay fixed. Which objects and relationships need checking varies by scene. p. 12 ↗

How GREP evaluates

A vision-language model reads the room and its objects. GREP turns checks into evidence, verdicts, and a report. Paper, p. 3 ↗

  1. 1

    Formulate checks

    Principled and adaptive.

    GREP derives checks for this room’s objects and relationships, including those the input description does not mention. p. 2 ↗

  2. 2

    Inspect & judge

    Evidence is proactive.

    For each check, GREP chooses measurements, existing views, or newly rendered views. p. 4 ↗

    It stops when evidence is sufficient or the acquisition budget is exhausted. Unresolved gaps remain in the report. p. 5 ↗

  3. 3

    Score & diagnose

    More than a number.

    The report links affected objects, reasons, and evidence to guide revision. p. 3 ↗ Revision study, p. 8 ↗
